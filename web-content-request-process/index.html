Content & Business Workflow

Copy Build Launch
1English Copy Edits

Business provides updates needed in English. Content (& Legal if needed) reviews and edits.

2English Copy Confirmed

Content aligns with business on English copy.

3English Stage Build

Content updates English stage build based on the confirmed English copy.

4English Stage Build Confirmed

Content aligns with business on English stage build.

5Translations

Content submits English stage build to Localization for translations, if needed.

6Confirmation & Go Live

Content aligns with business on translated stage build and goes live on business-preferred date.

FAQs

Translations

Can I provide preferred translations?

The provided translations must match the English copy 100%. Otherwise, Localization team will not be able to implement due to inaccuracy.

How to request changes to the translations provided by the Localization team?

If the change doesn’t match the source English, we will need to readjust English copy and build first.

If the source English is not impacted, please let us know why the change is required.

Assets

How to request asset (images) changes?

Please share with us your preferred asset from the Tesla Gallery or the Tesla website.
